Tips for a good photo
  • Photograph a single leaf, filling most of the frame.
  • Use a plain background (a sheet of paper or bare ground).
  • Shoot in daylight, avoid strong shadows or blur.
  • Include both healthy and affected areas of the leaf if possible.
