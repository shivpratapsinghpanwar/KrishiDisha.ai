About KrishiDisha

KrishiDisha is a decision-support platform for Indian farmers that combines machine learning models, live weather & market data, and a conversational AI assistant, so that agronomic decisions that once needed a visit to the Krishi Vigyan Kendra can start right from a phone.

About the project

KrishiDisha is built by a founding team of three — Abhishek Chourasia, Goutam Mandloi and Shivpratap Singh Panwar. The goal is straightforward: give an Indian farmer the same first-pass agronomic judgement a Krishi Vigyan Kendra visit would give, from a phone, in a language they speak, before they spend money on seed or fertilizer.

The team's guiding principle for the vision model is that only real field photographs of Indian crops count — lab-condition datasets like PlantVillage are excluded from training and from every accuracy number the app reports, so what is shown is what the model can actually do on a farmer's own photo. See the GitHub repository for the full source, training pipeline and model reports.

This application is the implementation of our paper "KrishiDisha: Revolutionizing Agriculture with Intelligent Recommendations, Disease Detection, and Yield Prediction", 2025 IEEE International Conference on Engineering Innovations and Technologies (ICoEIT), pp. 1028–1040, doi:10.1109/ICoEIT63558.2025.11211713. The work has moved on since publication: we are building the more advanced parts now — our own fine-tuned agronomy language model, support for the major Indian languages, and a farmer feedback loop that grows the field-photo dataset behind the disease model.

Datasets behind the models

Crop_recommendation.csv

2,200 rows covering 22 crops, with soil N-P-K, temperature, humidity, pH and rainfall.

Fertilizer Prediction.csv

Soil and crop parameters mapped to the recommended fertilizer type.

crop_yield.csv

Yield history across 55 crops and 30 Indian states, used to train the yield predictor.

PlantVillage

Leaf-image dataset with 38 disease/healthy classes, used to train the disease-detection CNN.
