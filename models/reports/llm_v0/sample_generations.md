# Sample generations

**User (hi):** धान के लिए कैसा मौसम चाहिए?

**Model:** धान (जलवायु): Warm humid climate, 21-30 C, sensitive to frost and waterlogging.

---

**User (hinglish):** dhaan ke liye kaisa climate chahiye?

**Model:** dhaan ke liye climate: Warm humid climate, 24-37 C, sensitive to waterlogging and frost.

---

**User (en):** What soil type and pH suits rice?

**Model:** For rice, soil: Wide adaptability including paddy fields, light soils to peat up to 20% clay, pH 6.0-7.5.

---

**User (hinglish):** dhaan ke liye kaunsi mitti best hai?

**Model:** dhaan ke liye soil: Well-drained loam to clay loam, pH 6.0-7.5.

---

**User (en):** NPK dose for rice?

**Model:** For rice, fertilizer schedule: 120:60:40 NPK kg/ha; half N and full P,K basal, remaining N at first irrigation (21-28 DAS). Confirm doses with your nearest KVK or a soil test.

---

**User (en):** When and how should I harvest rice?

**Model:** For rice, harvest: 100-120 days after transplanting; harvest at physiological maturity (50-60% grain moisture).

---

**User (hi):** धान की पैदावार कितनी होती है?

**Model:** धान (पैदावार): 15-20 q/ha rainfed; 25-30 q/ha irrigated hybrid.

---

**User (hi):** मक्का के लिए कैसा मौसम चाहिए?

**Model:** मक्का (जलवायु): Warm frost-free period 18-27 C for 20-225 days depending on hybrid, Kharif: June-July nursery, Oct-Nov transplant.

---

**User (en):** What soil type and pH suits maize?

**Model:** Maize (soil): Wide adaptability including light, saline/alkaline and marginal soils, pH up to 9.5 tolerated.

---

**User (hinglish):** makka me spacing kitni rakhein?

**Model:** makka ke liye spacing: Rows 90-120 cm apart; up to 150 cm for narrow hybrids.

---

**User (en):** NPK dose for maize?

**Model:** Maize (fertilizer schedule): 100-120:60:40 NPK kg/ha; half N and full P,K basal, remaining N in two splits at knee-high and tasseling. Confirm doses with your nearest KVK or a soil test.

---

**User (hi):** मक्का की उन्नत किस्में कौन सी हैं?

**Model:** मक्का (उन्नत किस्में): Pusa Rohini, Pusa 3416, DWRB 108, Pusa Riddhi, BSMR 1009.
