# Sample generations

**User (hinglish):** dhaan ke liye kaisa climate chahiye?

**Model:** dhaan ke liye climate: Warm humid climate, 20-30 C, sensitive to frost and waterlogging.

---

**User (en):** What soil type and pH suits rice?

**Model:** For rice, soil: Well-drained loam to clay loam, pH 6.0-7.0; avoid waterlogging-prone soils.

---

**User (hinglish):** dhaan ke liye kaunsi mitti best hai?

**Model:** dhaan ke liye soil: Well-drained loam to clay loam, pH 6.0-7.5.

---

**User (en):** NPK dose for rice?

**Model:** For rice, fertilizer schedule: 120:60:40 NPK kg/ha plus 20-40 kg S/ha; half N and full P,K,S basal, remaining N at first irrigation. Confirm doses with your nearest KVK or a soil test.

---

**User (en):** When and how should I harvest rice?

**Model:** For rice, harvest: 120-140 days after transplanting; harvest at physiological maturity when 80% grains turn brown and dry.

---

**User (en):** What soil type and pH suits maize?

**Model:** Maize (soil): Wide adaptability, sandy loam to clay loam, well-drained, pH 6.0-7.5.

---

**User (hinglish):** makka me spacing kitni rakhein?

**Model:** makka ke liye spacing: 30-45 cm x 10-15 cm.

---

**User (en):** NPK dose for maize?

**Model:** For maize, fertilizer schedule: 120:60:40 NPK kg/ha plus 20-30 kg S/ha; half N and full P,K,S basal, remaining N at first irrigation. Confirm doses with your nearest KVK or a soil test.

---

**User (en):** Which season is jute sown in?

**Model:** For jute, season: Kharif crop, a long-duration (180-270 days) fibre crop.

---

**User (hinglish):** jute me per hectare kitna beej lagega?

**Model:** jute ke liye seed rate: 15-20 kg/ha broadcast sowing; 8-10 kg/ha line sowing. Sahi dose ke liye apne KVK ya soil test se confirm karein.

---

**User (en):** Which improved varieties of jute should I grow?

**Model:** For jute, improved varieties: JU 148, JU 163, JU 179, JU 184, JU 191.

---

**User (en):** Which soil is best for cotton?

**Model:** For cotton, soil: Sandy loam to sandy soil, well-drained, pH 6.0-7.5.
