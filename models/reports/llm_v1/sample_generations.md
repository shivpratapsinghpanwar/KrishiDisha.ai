# Sample generations

**User (hinglish):** dhaan ke liye kaisa climate chahiye?

**Model:** dhaan ke liye climate: Cool growing period 18-22 C, warm dry weather at grain filling improves quality.

---

**User (en):** What soil type and pH suits rice?

**Model:** For rice, soil: Well-drained loam to clay loam, pH 6.0-7.0; avoid waterlogging-prone areas.

---

**User (hinglish):** dhaan ke liye kaunsi mitti best hai?

**Model:** dhaan ke liye soil: Well-drained loam to clay loam, pH 6.0-7.5.

---

**User (en):** NPK dose for rice?

**Model:** Rice (fertilizer schedule): 100:50:50 NPK kg/ha; full P2O5 and K2O at transplanting, additional N at tillering and panicle initiation. Confirm doses with your nearest KVK or a soil test.

---

**User (en):** When and how should I harvest rice?

**Model:** For rice, harvest: 100-120 days; harvest at physiological maturity (grain moisture 20-24%) for dry rice.

---

**User (en):** What soil type and pH suits maize?

**Model:** Maize (soil): Well-drained sandy loam to loam, pH 6.0-7.5.

---

**User (hinglish):** makka me spacing kitni rakhein?

**Model:** makka ke liye spacing: 30 cm x 10 cm.

---

**User (en):** NPK dose for maize?

**Model:** For maize, fertilizer schedule: 60-80:30-40:20 NPK kg/ha; full P and K plus 1/3 N basal, remaining N in two splits at knee-high and tasseling. Confirm doses with your nearest KVK or a soil test.

---

**User (en):** Which season is jute sown in?

**Model:** Jute (season): Kharif crop, a major plantation crop of West Bengal, Bihar and Assam.

---

**User (hinglish):** jute me per hectare kitna beej lagega?

**Model:** jute ke liye seed rate: 16-20 kg/ha broadcast; 8-10 kg/ha line sowing. Sahi dose ke liye apne KVK ya soil test se confirm karein.

---

**User (en):** Which improved varieties of jute should I grow?

**Model:** For jute, improved varieties: JU 101, JU 104, JU 106, JU 107, JU 108.

---

**User (en):** Which soil is best for cotton?

**Model:** For cotton, soil: Sandy loam to sandy soil, well-drained, pH 6.0-7.5.
